# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the ErikrafT Drop™ project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

We expect contributors, maintainers, users, and community members to communicate constructively and to focus on the technical work, documentation, and community around the project.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, documentation, or behavior rather than people.
- Respecting different levels of experience and technical backgrounds.
- Providing relevant context when reporting bugs or proposing changes.
- Accepting that maintainers may decline or request changes to a contribution.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech or demeaning comments directed at individuals or groups.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual content.
- Publishing private or sensitive information about another person without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions, issue trackers, pull requests, or community spaces.
- Malicious behavior intended to compromise project infrastructure, accounts, or other users.
- Other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

Possible actions include removing or editing content, closing discussions, restricting participation, or taking other measures appropriate to the situation.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns involving the project, please contact the maintainers privately:

- **Email:** [contact+security@erikraft.com](mailto:contact+security@erikraft.com)
- **Discord:** use the `#ticket` channel at [discord.erikraft.com](https://discord.erikraft.com/)

For security vulnerabilities, please follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use the private security-reporting channel described there.

Reports should include enough context for maintainers to understand what happened. Please avoid publicly posting private or sensitive information.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, code reviews, documentation, and project-related community spaces when they are used for ErikrafT Drop™ activities.
