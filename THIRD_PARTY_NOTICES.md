## Cimbar research reference

The ErikrafT Optical Matrix implementation is new browser code and does not
include Cimbar, libcimbar, CFC, their assets, or their dependencies. Its
technical research reference was the Cimbar commit
`7b0ab1d46f84f012887b4f424c5a7ca9f0479d87`, which is MIT licensed:

Copyright (c) 2020 Stephen Zimmerman

The reference project describes a 16-symbol, colour-icon matrix, Reed-Solomon
error correction, interleaving, and optional fountain streams. No source or
sample asset was copied into this repository. Cimbar and CFC compatibility is
therefore not asserted.
